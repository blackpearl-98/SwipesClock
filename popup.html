Privacy & swipe access

SwipeClock reads today’s swipe dates, times, and IN/OUT directions from greytHR to calculate your work and break time. It uses your existing greytHR session to request those records.

Swipe data stays in Chrome local storage. Daily target preferences may sync through Chrome Sync. SwipeClock does not send data to its own server.
